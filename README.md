## Entry

> One star. He unrolls my mat every morning, copies my stretches, and calls it yoga. The pose is named after me. He still does it wrong.

A deadpan one-star review from a house cat about its human — 134 characters, one line.

## Why this angle

The complaint is specific and instantly recognisable — the human's morning yoga routine — and it is judged entirely by cat standards: the mat is the cat's, the stretches are the cat's, and the yoga pose is literally named after the cat. The sting lands in the last sentence.
